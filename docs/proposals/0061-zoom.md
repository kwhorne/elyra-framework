# RFC 0061 — Zoom: a window on a long series

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-27) · **Target:** 0.61.0 · builds on [RFC 0058](0058-elyra-charts.md), [RFC 0059](0059-all-of-wirecharts.md) and [RFC 0060](0060-exporting-a-chart.md) · see [Decisions](#decisions)

## Summary

A year of daily sales is 365 points. Drawn whole, it's a smudge; the
question is usually about one month. WireCharts' `LineTimeChart`,
`SplineTimeChart` and `LineBoostChart` take `zoom` and get ECharts' slider
and wheel zoom; the six charts of 0.58 have nothing, and nothing in
`@elyra/charts` lets the app know or set what's shown, or reach it from the
keyboard.

This RFC gives every chart with a category or time axis a zoom the user
drives with the pointer or the keys, and the app reads and sets:

```svelte
<LineChart {series} categories={days} zoom bind:range />

<p>Showing {range[0]} – {range[1]}</p>
<Button onclick={() => (range = ["2026-03-01", "2026-03-31"])}>March</Button>
<Button onclick={() => (range = null)}>All</Button>
```

- **`zoom`** turns it on for `LineChart`, `AreaChart`, `ColumnChart`,
  `BarChart` and `ScatterChart` (and stays WireCharts' `zoom` on the
  three that have it): the wheel and a pinch zoom, a drag pans, and a slider
  under the chart shows where the window is.
- **`bind:range`** is the window as the axis's own values (`["2026-03-01",
  "2026-03-31"]`, or `["Q2", "Q3"]`), or `null` for all of it: read it to
  filter a table beside the chart, set it to jump.
- **The keyboard**: with the chart focused, `+` and `-` zoom about the
  focused category, ←/→ pan when zoomed (they still walk the categories,
  and the window follows), `0` shows all. The slider's handles are
  focusable, with their values as names.
- **What it says** follows the window: the summary is of what's shown
  ("Revenue in March: from 120 to 260…"), and the hidden table keeps every
  row, marking the shown ones, so the assistant can answer about any month.
- **Live data keeps the window**: new points don't reset it; a window at
  the end of the axis follows the end ("the last 30 days").
- **Export takes what's shown** (RFC 0060): the image is the window; the
  CSV, the whole series.

## Motivation

- **Long series are the desktop's.** An app over a database draws years
  of rows; the web's dashboards page them, a desktop chart should zoom.
- **The window is state.** "Show March" from a button, the table beside
  the chart filtered to the same dates, the window kept as the user left
  it (`persist`, as `Table`'s widths are): all need the app to have it.
- **A slider isn't enough for the keyboard.** ECharts' handles aren't in the
  tab order; a chart that zooms only by the pointer isn't one a keyboard
  user can read a month of.

## Current state (0.60)

- WireCharts' `lineTime`, `splineTime` and `lineBoost` builders add
  `dataZoom: [{ type: "inside" }, { type: "slider" }]` when `zoom` is
  true: the pointer only; the app doesn't learn the window.
- The six of 0.58 (`internal/options.js`) have no `dataZoom`; ←/→ walk the
  categories with the tooltip (RFC 0058).
- ECharts reports the window on `datazoom` (as percents or values) and takes
  it from `dispatchAction({ type: "dataZoom", startValue, endValue })`.

## Design

### The props

| Prop | | |
|---|---|---|
| `zoom` | `false` | The wheel, a pinch, a drag and the slider. |
| `range` (bindable) | `null` | The window, as `[from, to]` in the axis's values, or `null` for all. |
| `persist` | — | A key: the window is kept in the app's settings store and comes back. |

On the six, `zoom` adds ECharts' `inside` and `slider` data zooms on the
category axis (the value axis, for `ScatterChart`'s x), styled from the
tokens: the slider's track `--elyra-inset`, its window `--elyra-accent-soft`,
its handles `--elyra-accent`, its labels `--elyra-muted`. On a WireCharts
chart with `zoom`, the same styling and the same `range`.

### The window, both ways

ChartBase listens to `datazoom` and sets `range` to the axis's values at the
window's ends (ECharts gives percents for the slider and values for the
wheel; both are turned into values). Setting `range` dispatches `dataZoom`
with `startValue`/`endValue`. `null` is `0`–`100` percent. A window that
reached the axis's end follows it when live data adds points.

### The keyboard

On the chart's focus (one tab stop, as now):

- `+` / `=` and `-` zoom in and out by half, about the focused category (or
  the window's middle);
- ←/→ walk the categories as now; past the window's edge, the window pans
  to keep the focused one in it;
- `0` shows all;
- Shift+←/→ pan the window by a tenth.

The slider's handles get `role="slider"`, `aria-valuetext` (the end's value,
in the locale), and ←/→ of their own, through an overlay ChartBase draws
over ECharts' slider, so a screen reader and the keyboard reach them.

### What it says

The summary is computed from the points in the window, prefixed by it
(`elyra.charts.window`: "Revenue, 1 March – 31 March: from 120 to 260…").
The table keeps all rows; the shown ones are marked
(`aria-current="true"` on their row header), and its caption says the
window. Export (RFC 0060): the image is what's drawn; the CSV, every row.

### Testing

- The option: `dataZoom` with the tokens' colours when `zoom`; none without.
- `range` in: the `dataZoom` action with values; `null`: all. `range` out:
  from a `datazoom` event with percents and with values.
- Live: a window at the end follows the end; one in the middle stays.
- The keys: `+`/`-`/`0`, ←/→ past the edge, Shift+←/→.
- The summary of the window; the table marking it; `persist`.
- A browser check: wheel and slider in the gallery, the window in the
  summary.

## Implementation plan

1. `zoom`, `range` and the styling in ChartBase and the six; WireCharts'
   three through the same.
2. The keys and the slider overlay.
3. The summary, the table's marks, `persist`; export.
4. The gallery's time charts zoomable; docs, CHANGELOG.

## Open questions

1. **Off by default?**
   *Recommendation:* yes. A chart of twelve months doesn't need it, and a
   wheel over a dashboard should scroll the page, not zoom a chart.
2. **The slider always, or only the wheel and the keys?**
   *Recommendation:* the slider with `zoom`: it's where the window is
   seen, and what a pointer user without a wheel drags.
3. **`range` in the axis's values, or as percents?**
   *Recommendation:* values. "March" is what an app means; percents
   change meaning when live data adds a day.
4. **The table: all rows, or only the window's?**
   *Recommendation:* all rows, the window marked. The assistant answering
   "and April?" shouldn't depend on where the user left the slider.
5. **Zoom on the value axis too (a y zoom)?**
   *Recommendation:* not in this RFC; the category or time axis is where
   the length is.

## Decisions

1. **Off by default**; `zoom` turns it on.
2. **The slider always with `zoom`**, beside the wheel, a pinch, a drag and
   the keys.
3. **`range` in the axis's values**, `null` for all.
4. **The table keeps every row**, the window's marked.
5. **No zoom on the value axis** in this RFC.

## Not in this RFC

- **Brushing** (selecting points with a rectangle), and linked charts
  (one chart's window driving another's) beyond `bind:range`.
- **Zoom on the y axis**, on pies, gauges, trees and the like.

## Alternatives considered

- **Only WireCharts' `zoom` on more charts**: the pointer gets it; the app
  and the keyboard don't.
- **Paging the data instead** (`Pagination` under a chart): a month at a
  time loses the year's shape, which is why one zooms.
